# Data Access
All data from the Skypallet's instruments will be made available as soon as possible after the cruises. Access links to the individual datasets will be added here in due time.

# Data Policy
We encourage free use and open access to the data from Skypallet observations, and we rely on the integrity of the scientific community to ensure that this open data policy does not diminish the contributions of those who collected these data.

Skypallet data are the product of the collective efforts of many scientists, often supported by external funding agencies. The data represent years of work in developing and operating instruments, often tied to specific research or Ph.D. projects. We ask users to respect the priority of the data collectors by coordinating their use of Skypallet data with the Principal Investigators (PIs) responsible for the data collection, and by clearly acknowledging the source of the data in all publications and presentations. Contact details for the relevant PIs will be provided alongside each dataset.

For publications using Skypallet data, please include this acknowledgement:

> “The operation of the Skypallet was made possible thanks to the support of Polhavet 2050, the European Research Council through ERC Consolidator Grant "STEP-CHANGE" (grant number 101045273), and the University of Oslo’s Cold Climate Container Facilities. [The specific data used in this study were made available by institution/person XY, with partial support from agency XYZ.]

This is to ensure the recognition of contributions to the data collection, while promoting open and respectful use of the data.
